# Greek Gridiron — Player Roster Form

Field-by-field content, ready to paste into Jotform, Google Forms, or similar. Submitted once per team by the team captain (not individually by each player) — the captain's signature on the Team Registration Form already covers the whole roster, so no separate per-player signatures are collected here. Due **~September 19, 2026** (1 week before the event).

## ⚠️ Open item before publishing this form
- Confirm this form is linked to/gated behind a completed **Team Registration Form** submission (same chapter name should match what was entered there).

---

## Section 1: Team Info
- **Chapter/Organization Name** (required — must match the name used on the Team Registration Form)
- **Bracket** (required, radio): ○ Men's Bracket ○ Women's Bracket
- **Captain Name** (required — for cross-reference with the Team Registration Form)

## Section 2: Roster
Repeating row, one per player (7–10 rows required — see roster size limits below):

| Field | Required? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Player Full Name | Required | Must be an initiated, active member of the chapter |
| Phone or Email | Optional | For day-of contact if needed |
| Jersey/Flag Number | Optional | Helps referees identify players; not required for 7v7 flag football |

- **Roster size:** minimum 7, maximum 10 players.
- By submitting this form, the captain reaffirms the eligibility attestation made on the Team Registration Form — all listed players are initiated, active members of the chapter as of this submission.
