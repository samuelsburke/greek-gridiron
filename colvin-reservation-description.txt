EVENT DESCRIPTION — Greek Gridiron

Greek Gridiron is a one-day, 7-on-7 flag football tournament hosted by the Sigma Omicron chapter of Phi Gamma Delta (FIJI) at Oklahoma State University. The event is being organized as a philanthropic fundraiser, with proceeds benefiting For the Love of Orphans, a nonprofit organization based in Argyle, Texas. This will be the inaugural year of Greek Gridiron, which the chapter intends to establish as a recurring annual event within OSU's Greek community.

DATE AND TIME
Saturday, September 26, 2026, from 10:00 AM to 9:30 PM. All games, awards, and closing activities will conclude by 9:30 PM in compliance with the City of Stillwater's 10:00 PM noise ordinance.

FACILITY REQUEST
We are requesting the reservation of both practice fields located at the Colvin Recreation Center Annex for the full duration of the event. These fields will be used to run all pool play, playoff, and championship games across the day.

FORMAT AND STRUCTURE
The tournament will feature two parallel brackets: a Men's Bracket, open to Oklahoma State's Interfraternity Council (IFC) chapters, and a Women's Bracket, open to OSU's Panhellenic sorority chapters; each following an identical competitive format. Depending on final team registration, each bracket will run either an 8-team format (two pools of four, with the top two teams from each pool advancing to semifinals and a championship game) or a 10–12 team format (three pools of three to four teams, with top teams advancing through quarterfinals, semifinals, and a championship game).

Game lengths will scale with the tournament schedule: pool play games will consist of 20-minute running-clock halves, playoff games will consist of 25-minute halves, and the championship games for each bracket will consist of 30-minute halves.

PARTICIPANTS AND ATTENDANCE
We anticipate approximately 175 total attendees across the day, including players from participating fraternity and sorority chapters, spectators, and event volunteers/staff.

EVENT ACTIVITIES
In addition to the on-field competition, the event will include a small concessions operation: a soda bar and a snowcone machine, run by 2–3 chapter volunteers. The closing ceremony will recognize the beneficiary organization, present the philanthropic donation, and award tournament trophies for both the Men's and Women's brackets.

CONDUCT AND SAFETY
All participating chapters will be required to agree to a code of conduct before the event, covering sportsmanship toward opponents, officials, and event staff, with officials' decisions treated as final. The hosting chapter will be responsible for the conduct of its own participants and spectators throughout the event. Games will not be played during rain or when field conditions are poor, which aligns with standard Colvin field-use policy.

EQUIPMENT AND SETUP
No additional equipment, infrastructure, or facility services are being requested beyond use of the two practice fields; the hosting chapter will be self-sufficient for all event-day setup.
